Business Logic

How Your Investment & Returns Work

30-Day Cycle Activation

Once your investment is activated, the 30-day cycle begins from the day of deposit. After the full cycle is completed, your profit is credited automatically to your wallet balance.

Flat 7% Profit

Profit is always calculated on your active Current Investment only. This is a non-compounding model, meaning there is no extra growth added on previously earned profit unless it remains active as part of the current investment balance.

Capital Flexibility

You may keep your profit in the account without receiving additional interest. Your principal capital remains protected and continues to serve as the active investment base for the next cycle.

Smart Withdrawal Order

During any withdrawal request, the system automatically deducts from profit first, and if required, the remaining portion is deducted from capital. This keeps transactions transparent and orderly.

Investor FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I invest additional funds later?

Ans: Yes, you can make extra deposits at any time. For example: $300 + $230 = $530. Your next profit will be calculated at 7% on the full $530 investment.

Q: What is the withdrawal policy?

Ans: After 3 business days from the initial investment, you can submit a withdrawal request at any time. The system will deduct from profit first, and if needed, the remaining amount will be deducted from the capital.

Q: Will my accumulated profit be automatically compounded?

Ans: No. Even if profit is accumulated in your dashboard, the monthly 7% return applies only to your active Current Investment. This is a non-compounding model.

Q: Can I open an account without KYC?

Ans: No. This platform is designed for known and verified investors, so account creation and deposits/withdrawals are completed only after KYC verification is successfully completed.
